Why powers of 10? Each step up the power of 10 multiplies the quantity by 10. The difference between 10⁶ and 10⁹ is a factor of 1000 — not 3. A number's exponent tells you roughly how many digits it has. The Sun is 10¹¹ m away; the nearest star beyond the Sun is 10¹⁶ m away — 100,000 times further.
